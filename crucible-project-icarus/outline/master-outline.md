## Five Movements

### MOVEMENT I — IGNITION (10% · Chapters 1–2)
*The bridge to the ordinary world burns.*

The lower-decks world established. Mercy Moon as a pilot with hunger that exceeds his circumstances. Kweku arrives with prototype wings built as escape technology. Mercy defies Kweku's instruction and enters the first public race — the crowd learns his name and he loses his autonomy.

**Forge Point 0 (Ch 2):** First public race → crowd learns his name (Quest) → survives the impossible and grins (Fire) → someone from the crew taken as collateral (Constellation) → **Sacrifice: autonomy lost, conscripted by the empire**

---

### MOVEMENT II — FIRST TEMPERING (20% · Chapters 3–6)
*He learns to fly. The fellowship cracks.*

Mercy learns the wings and their cost. The tolerance curve begins. A rival flame (Bex) shows him the inside-the-system alternative. A major run earns Darius's personal attention. The empire's true nature revealed — manufactured addiction funding political consolidation. Community collateral makes the cost visible. Kweku reveals the history of the lost free era; Darius shown as the comprehensible path.

**Forge Point 1 (Ch 5):** Discovers empire's nature (Quest) → exceeds safe limit (Fire) → mentor confrontation, first real crack (Constellation) → **Sacrifice: exceeds the red line; body begins its accounting**

---

### MOVEMENT III — THE SCATTERING (25% · Chapters 7–11)
*He becomes the myth. The myth costs him.*

The constellation expands: Zel's underground signal and the activist network adopt Mercy as a symbol. Parallel missions running in background (Kweku's secret project; Juno alone). Ruthless choices harden him. Juno endangered by Darius as leverage. The full institutional rot revealed — Mercy is simultaneously the movement's symbol and its enemy. Mercy 2: a family from his wreckage. FP2: selfish full burn against Bex, Juno almost walks.

**Forge Point 2 (Ch 11):** Selfish full burn against Bex for the crowd (Fire) → mission loses ground (Quest) → Juno confronts him with both hidden things at once, almost walks (Constellation) → **Sacrifice: quest loses ground; the body cost undeniable**

---

### MOVEMENT IV — BRIGHTEST BURNING (25% · Chapters 12–16)
*Maximum velocity. Maximum loss.*

The illusion of mastery: two impossible things in one day. From peak altitude, Mercy sees the full shape of what's coming — the obsolescence tech and the completed political coup. Bex dies because Mercy didn't come back. Darius offers Mercy his life back; Mercy considers it. The mentor bond holds — Kweku tells the truth he's been carrying. Mercy refuses the offer. FP3: empire cuts him, replacement wings fly publicly, Mercy 3 (protects Zel), a movement organizer dies, Kweku's final plea.

**Forge Point 3 (Ch 16):** Empire cuts him + new wings fly publicly (Quest) → fire shows him what he's losing (Fire) → movement organizer dies; Kweku's final plea; Juno learns what he hid (Constellation) → **Sacrifice: the last operational resource spent protecting Zel**

---

### MOVEMENT V — FINAL FORGING (15% · Chapters 17–19)
*The fall everyone saw coming. He flies anyway.*

The final run: into Darius's territory, delivering Kweku's escape plan. Darius intercepts. Three failures — tactical (intercepted), personal (Juno endangered), spiritual (the doubt). The old kindnesses return in cascade: Bex's legacy opens a lane, the family covers Juno's position, Zel's signal turns the crowd into infrastructure. Mission completes. One transmission with Darius — the argument answered. Mercy climbs past any altitude anyone has reached, releases the anger, smiles before the wings fail, falls toward the sun.

**Forge Point Apex (Ch 19):** Lives before the solar system (Quest) → full burn, releases the need to be seen, flies toward the sun (Fire) → constellation watches the final run (Constellation) → **Surrender: the anger released; the smile is the proof**

---

### CODA — TEMPERED BLADE (5% · Chapter 20)
*The flame passes.*

Juno carries the accounting — who is gone, what changed, what didn't. The political fight isn't over but the architecture has shifted. Kweku in the workshop, building something new. Zel's signal still broadcasting. The Coda ends on an implied sound: someone is going to fly. The sky is open in a way it wasn't.
