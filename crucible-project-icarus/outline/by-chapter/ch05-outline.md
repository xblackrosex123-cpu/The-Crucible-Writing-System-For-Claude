# Chapter 5 — Afterburn

**Movement:** II — First Tempering
**Beats:** 11 (First Forge Point — FP1) · 12 (The Shaping Blow)
**Strand Focus:** Quest / Fire / Constellation — all three in crisis
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 5,000–6,000
**Forge Point:** FP1 — First Crucible

---

## Purpose

The first impossible choice lands. Mercy discovers the empire's true nature, exceeds the safe limit, and the mentor bond cracks for real. Then the Shaping Blow: community collateral makes the cost visible in a way that can't be reframed. The cost of being seen is in the same frame as the hunger for the first time.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 5.1 — The Meeting with Darius (FP1 setup)
Mercy meets Darius for the first time. Darius is precise, not theatrical — generous, even, in the way that people with all the power can afford to be.

The reveal: the drugs Mercy is running are manufactured addiction, targeted at the lower decks. The drug trade is the funding mechanism for a political consolidation play — the criminal syndicates are becoming permanent governance. Every run Mercy has completed has helped build this.

**Quest crisis:** He discovers what he's been running. He is not a smuggler. He is infrastructure for the empire that owns the lower decks.

Darius offers more — more runs, more sky, more money. More of everything. Phrased as opportunity.

**Turn:** Mercy leaves the meeting carrying the shape of what he's been doing.

### Scene 5.2 — The Red Line (Fire crisis)
On the run following the meeting — out of rage, defiance, or the need to feel something uncomplicated — Mercy pushes past the threshold Kweku showed him in Ch 4. The safe limit. The wings demand it; he gives it.

The run is extraordinary. Something happens in those seconds that nobody else in the solar system could do. The cost lands immediately and physically on the other side — worse than the shaking hands in Ch 3.

**Fire crisis:** He exceeded the safe limit and knows it. The body is paying in a new currency.

**Constellation crisis:** Kweku confronts him — not the careful argument from Ch 4, the real one. *"You knew. You did it anyway."* The first real crack in the mentor bond: not disappointment but recognition, which is harder.

**Sacrifice:** The body has begun its accounting. The safe limit is behind him.

**Turn:** The confrontation ends without resolution. The crack is in the bond now.

### Scene 5.3 — The Shaping Blow
The collateral damage arrives: a lower-decks community Mercy ran through — repeatedly, at speed, the pressure differential from prototype wings tearing through residential corridors — has been targeted. Not by Darius directly. As a consequence of Mercy's visibility, the syndicate asserting control over territory he made active. People he knew. A place that was ordinary and is now not.

Mercy sees this and understands the connection. Not simple fault — but his becoming-a-legend has a wake, and people in that wake get hurt.

**Turn:** The hunger and the cost are in the same frame. He sees both. He doesn't stop. But the seeing is irreversible.

## Tone
Three distinct registers. The Darius meeting: precise, almost pleasant, the horror in the information not the delivery. The red-line run: pure kinetic violence against physics. The shaping blow: quiet. The community damage should land without editorializing — just the facts of what Mercy's wake looks like from the ground.
