# Chapter 2 — Echo Location

**Movement:** I — Ignition
**Beats:** 3 (The First Spark) · 4 (The Burden Chosen) · 5 (The First Constellation) · 6 (The Door That Burns — FP0)
**Strand Focus:** Fire / Quest / Constellation — all three hit
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 5,000–6,000
**Forge Point:** FP0 — Ignition

---

## Purpose

Complete the opening movement: the wings revealed, the fire ignited, the burden accepted with incomplete understanding, the first constellation formed — and then the door burned behind him by his own choice. FP0 ends the chapter and the ordinary world simultaneously.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 2.1 — The Wings
Kweku's workshop. The prototype wings. His own creation — years of work, never shown to anyone. Not designed for running cargo for the syndicates. Designed for escape: break the conglomerate's monopoly on the flight lanes, open a route out. The wings are the free era's last artifact, built from its failure.

First flight: Mercy in the wings for the first time (contained — workshop, short test corridor). The Fire erupts on contact. He grins. This is what he was made for. The wings and the hunger are the same shape.

**Turn:** Kweku watches. He's built the thing. He knows what he's watching.

### Scene 2.2 — The Burden Chosen
Kweku lays out the terms: secret, patient, disciplined. This is a long game, not a spectacle. Mercy agrees.

He hears *fly the fastest thing ever built.* He doesn't hear *stay hidden.*

The burden accepted with incomplete understanding — this is the central tragic gap, established here. The gap between the mission Kweku offered and the mission Mercy accepted will drive every Forge Point that follows.

**Turn:** Mercy says yes. The wings have a rider.

### Scene 2.3 — First Constellation
Juno finds out. She commits. The ground crew — small, lower-decks, bound by trust and proximity — forms around the wings. The first constellation, not because anyone chose it formally but because these are the people who show up.

**Turn:** The crew is in. The wings have a ground team.

### Scene 2.4 — The Public Run (FP0)
Against Kweku's explicit warning, Mercy enters an underground race. The crowd is there. The wings will show them who he is.

**Quest crisis:** He wins. Impossibly. The crowd learns his name. *"Mercy Moon."*

**Fire crisis:** He survives something that should have killed him. He grins — the hunger satisfied for the first time. It feels like proof.

**Constellation crisis:** Someone from the crew is taken as collateral by Darius's people, who were watching.

**Sacrifice:** His autonomy. The wings have been seen. He's conscripted.

**Turn:** The grin fades when the message arrives. He made this choice himself. That's the worst part.

## Tone
The chapter should accelerate. The workshop scene is intimate and precise. The public run is pure kinetics. FP0's aftermath lands in silence — the grin fading, the message arriving, the door burned. The reader should feel the hinge: everything before this chapter is prelude. Everything after is consequence.
