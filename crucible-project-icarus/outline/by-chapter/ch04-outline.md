# Chapter 4 — Sun Protocol

**Movement:** II — First Tempering
**Beats:** 9 (First Test of Quest) · 10 (Constellation Strained)
**Strand Focus:** Quest / Constellation
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 4,000–5,000

---

## Purpose

The false summit: a significant obstacle overcome, producing confidence that isn't warranted. Darius's attention earned. The mentor crack deepens — Kweku runs out of argument and falls into silence. The invitation to meet Darius arrives.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 4.1 — The Impossible Run
A major syndicate cargo run — the kind that requires a fleet. Mercy does it alone. Three points of failure encountered and improvised through. He lands clean. The lower decks celebrate in their way; the syndicate network registers the anomaly.

False confidence: Mercy reads this as proof he's ready for bigger. The body held this time. The wings held. He's not reading the tolerance curve in this moment — he's reading the win.

Darius's network logs the run. An anomaly worth investigating personally.

**Turn:** Darius's message arrives: a meeting requested. Phrased politely. Both parties understand it isn't optional.

### Scene 4.2 — Kweku's Data
The confrontation Kweku has been building toward. He has data — the wings' power draw per run, cross-referenced against what the body can sustain over time. The tolerance curve bends toward a hard limit, and the limit is not far.

Kweku is precise, not gentle. He shows Mercy the numbers. *This is what happens when you push past this threshold.*

Mercy hears the words. He just proved the wings. He just earned Darius's attention. The data is true and irrelevant in this moment.

The crack: Kweku stops arguing. That's worse than an argument — it's the silence of a man who has accepted he can't stop what's coming and is deciding how to carry that.

**Turn:** Kweku stops. Mercy goes to the meeting.

### Scene 4.3 — The Message from Darius
Brief closing scene. The invitation. The implicit understanding that there's no choice.

Mercy going anyway — not because he's forced, but because he wants to see. The hunger and the empire are converging on the same point.

**Turn:** He goes. The chapter closes on the approach.

---

## Chapter Turn
**Type:** Decision point
**The decision:** Mercy accepts Darius's meeting. He's choosing the sun.

## Tone
The run should feel triumphant — Mercy at his best, improvising, the wings doing what they were built for. Then Kweku's data scene is cold and precise. Two different registers in the same chapter: the high of the run and the quiet after, where the numbers say something the high can't hear.
