# Chapter 10 — The Soft Thing

**Movement:** III — The Scattering
**Beats:** 20 (Mercy That Costs)
**Strand Focus:** Constellation / Fire
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 3,500–4,500
**Mercy Engine:** Mercy 2 — the Family

---

## Purpose

Beat 20 in full: compassion where ruthlessness is easier. After the hardening of Ch 8 and the architectural revelation of Ch 9, this chapter shows that Mercy hasn't lost the soft thing yet. He stops when he doesn't have to. He pays the real cost. He notes it. He doesn't regret it.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 10.1 — The Family
Mid-run, Mercy passes through a sector he's used before — repeatedly, at speed. He sees what that speed costs from the ground: a family displaced, their home structurally damaged by the pressure differential his prototype wings generate at full power.

He knows this wreckage. He made it. Not this family specifically — but his runs have been making wreckage like this, and he's been moving past it.

He slows. He sees them clearly.

**Turn:** He stops.

### Scene 10.2 — The Soft Thing
He pulls up short of the delivery window. Gets the family out — uses the run's logistics, contacts, access to move them somewhere the pressure differential won't reach. It takes time he doesn't have. It costs a delivery window.

This is not the Mercy who ran the math in Ch 8. That Mercy chose the window. This Mercy doesn't run the math.

He doesn't explain himself. He just does it. When Juno gets the late-arrival notification, she covers the window as best she can. She doesn't ask what happened. She knows already.

**Turn:** The delivery is late. Darius's network makes a note. A note in a ledger becomes something else in a few chapters.

### Scene 10.3 — The Cost
Juno absorbs the fallout without complaint. That's the part that stings — not the operational consequence, but the weight Juno is carrying silently.

The chapter closes on Mercy: the family gone, the sector quiet, a delivery somewhere sitting unfinished. He doesn't regret it. He notes what it costs. Both things are true.

**Turn:** He still has softness. He doesn't know yet if that's a strength or a wound.

## Tone
The shortest chapter in Movement III — clean and precise. The soft thing shouldn't be dramatized or sentimentalized. Mercy stops, he does the thing, he pays the cost, he notes it, the chapter ends. The understatement is the point: this is who he still is underneath the hardening. The reader should feel the contrast with Ch 8 without the text pointing at it.
