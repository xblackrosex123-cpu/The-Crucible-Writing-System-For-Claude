# Chapter 3 — Static and Mirror

**Movement:** II — First Tempering
**Beats:** 7 (The Wandering Flame) · 8 (The Rival Flame)
**Strand Focus:** Fire / Constellation
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 4,000–5,000

---

## Purpose

Open the tempering with the two warnings Mercy won't take: the body already paying, and the rival who shows what the inside looks like. Both warnings register and are set aside. The Fire's cost is real and growing; the cost of the alternative is visible and ignored.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 3.1 — The Wandering Flame
Movement II opens mid-run. The wings misbehave — not catastrophically, but wrong. The tolerance is already building: the first run took a fraction of what this one demands. The body is paying a note Mercy can't fully read yet — hands shaking on landing, a gap in the memory of the fastest thirty seconds.

Juno runs diagnostics on the wings. She says nothing about his hands. She has the data. She's waiting to see if he'll notice on his own.

Mercy reframes: the wings breaking in, finding their calibration. Kweku, present and quiet, knows that's not what's happening.

**Turn:** The warning is in the data. Mercy doesn't take it.

### Scene 3.2 — Bex
Mercy crosses paths with Bex — a sanctioned racer with Darius's endorsement, upper-lane access, and crowds that cheer with permission. Everything Mercy has at the margins, Bex has at the center.

Bex is not a villain. They're legitimately skilled and genuinely likable, and they chose the inside of the system without apparent regret. They don't think they sold anything. The encounter is brief — a shared corridor, a moment of professional recognition between two pilots who both know exactly what the other is.

Mercy's hunger spikes on contact. Not just rivalry — a question: *is that available to me? Would I want it if it was?*

The warning, visible but untaken: Bex flies beautifully and doesn't own a thing. Every race has a sponsor. Every win belongs to someone else.

**Turn:** Mercy wants what Bex has AND doesn't want what Bex accepted. The hunger clarifies.

## Chapter Turn
**Type:** Revelation
**The revelation:** Mercy sees what the sky looks like from inside the system. He doesn't want it. But the wanting is more complicated than he thought.

## Tone
Two beats, two textures. The run is tight and physical — the wings misbehaving should feel like a car losing grip, present and alarming. The Bex encounter is quieter: two people in the same space, both reading the other, neither saying what they're actually seeing.
