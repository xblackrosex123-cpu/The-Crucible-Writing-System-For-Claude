# Chapter 1 — Low Light Gospel

**Movement:** I — Ignition
**Beats:** 1 (The Unlit Forge) · 2 (The Dual Arrival) · 3 (The First Spark)
**Strand Focus:** Constellation setup / Fire awakening
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 4,000–5,000

---

## Purpose

Establish Mercy Moon in his world before the world changes. Show the Fire before it has a name. Introduce Kweku without the wings. Plant the opening frame so the reader carries the weight of the fall through everything that follows.

---

## Opening

The novel opens on the frame: wreckage near the sun. Debris lit by the corona. A crowd watching from lower decks, from screens, from ships in the lanes. Something impossible and irreversible has happened. No name. No face. The myth already complete. Hard cut: *"Six years earlier."*

---

## Scenes

### Scene 1.1 — The Run
Mercy Moon in a cockpit. Juno running pre-flight checks. Small illegal cargo run — the margins of the lower decks, everything the syndicates don't own yet.

The world established through texture: flight lanes owned and taxed overhead, lower decks scraping the territory that doesn't turn a profit, Mercy too good for this work and clearly aware of it.

The run: he's precise and fast. Then he takes an unnecessary detour through a restricted corridor — not for efficiency, for a platform crowd watching below. A moment of visibility, brief and unauthorized. Someone on that platform notices. He doesn't do it for the cargo fee.

**Turn:** He lands. He got paid. He's not satisfied.

### Scene 1.2 — Ground After
Post-run on the lower decks. The hunger visible in how Mercy carries himself down the ramp. Juno and Mercy's dynamic established: she sees him clearly, she's been seeing him for a while, she doesn't soften what she sees, she shows up anyway. The Fire at rest — not named, just a restlessness that has no outlet at this scale.

**Turn:** Juno says nothing about the restricted detour. Her silence is not approval.

### Scene 1.3 — Kweku
An older engineer is already there — or arrives. Kweku. He watched the run. He doesn't explain himself immediately.

He tells Mercy there's something he needs to see. A project, hidden. Something that changes what "flying" means. He doesn't call it wings.

**Turn:** Mercy follows Kweku. A door closes behind them. The chapter ends before what's inside is shown.

---

## Chapter Turn
**Type:** Question raised
**The question:** What is Kweku hiding, and why does it feel like the beginning of something?

---

## Key Plants
- The wreckage in the frame is the same ship we see in Ch 19 — the reader won't know this until they finish
- The restricted detour plants the hunger as a pattern: unnecessary risk, taken for the crowd
- Juno's economy of movement establishes she's been watching Mercy a long time
- Kweku watching the run plants the mentor bond — he's been watching before Mercy knew

---

## Tone
Lower-decks texture. Kinetic but grounded. The world should feel alive and specific — the political geography of who owns what corridor, the sounds and smells of a ship returning. The Fire is present as absence: the run that should satisfy and doesn't.
