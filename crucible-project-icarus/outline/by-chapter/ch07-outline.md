# Chapter 7 — Sympathetic Frequencies

**Movement:** III — The Scattering
**Beats:** 15 (Constellation Expands) · 16 (Burdens Distributed)
**Strand Focus:** Constellation / Quest
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 4,500–5,500

---

## Purpose

Open the Scattering with Mercy's world expanding in ways he didn't choose. The legend has become larger than the pilot. Two parallel missions begin running beneath his attention — Kweku's unnamed project, Juno managing alone. Mercy doesn't notice what he doesn't know.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 7.1 — Zel's Signal
An underground broadcaster named Zel has been documenting the runs — low-fi signal, lower-decks distribution, the kind of thing Darius's network can't kill because it lives in the gaps. They found Mercy Moon before Mercy found them.

The activist network follows: already organizing, already named, already with a cause. They've adopted Mercy as a symbol without asking him. He's not their leader. He's their evidence. Proof the lanes can be broken.

Mercy's reaction: complicated. He wanted the sky to know his name — but he meant it as a personal thing, a pilot's achievement. This is political. The crowd is bigger now. The hunger responds to it anyway.

**Turn:** Mercy Moon is now larger than Mercy. He hasn't felt the difference yet.

### Scene 7.2 — Expanded Constellation
The interface between Mercy's crew and the new arrivals is managed by Juno — wary, efficient, keeping the operational from bleeding into the political. She's doing this alone. Kweku is present but elsewhere in his thinking.

Mercy floats through all of it. The legend is growing. He's at the center of something. He likes it.

### Scene 7.3 — Parallel Missions
Two things running beneath Mercy's attention:

Kweku has a project he hasn't named — working on something after hours, something that has nothing to do with the current runs. He works on it when Mercy is in the air. Mercy doesn't ask what it is.

Juno is making operational decisions alone as Mercy becomes increasingly present-but-absent. He's there in body; elsewhere in the myth he's becoming. She's carrying the crew's actual day-to-day without credit or acknowledgment. She doesn't ask for either.

Neither tells Mercy the full shape of what they're doing. He doesn't notice that he doesn't know.

**Turn:** The chapter ends with Mercy in the air again, the legend growing below him, the two people who built his ground making plans he can't see.
