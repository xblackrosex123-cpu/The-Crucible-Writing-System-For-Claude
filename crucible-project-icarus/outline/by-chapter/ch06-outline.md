# Chapter 6 — The Old Sky

**Movement:** II — First Tempering
**Beats:** 13 (The Sanctuary) · 14 (Dark Mirror Shown)
**Strand Focus:** Fire / Constellation
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 4,000–5,000

---

## Purpose

Close Movement II with the two things Mercy now carries: the history of what was possible and lost, and the recognition that the antagonist is not an external force but a possible destination. The dark mirror established — not as villain but as warning.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 6.1 — The Sanctuary
Kweku takes Mercy somewhere he hasn't taken anyone in a long time. A derelict sector — a place the conglomerates let decay because there's nothing to monetize. It was part of the free era. It remembers.

Kweku was part of the generation that tried to break the monopoly and failed. The wings are what he built from the wreckage of that failure. The people he lost are in this place — not literally, but in the details, the specific architecture of a time when the flight lanes weren't owned.

He doesn't lecture. He shows. The history arrives through what Mercy sees and what Kweku doesn't have to explain. What was possible. What was taken. What it cost the people who tried.

The wings become a different object by the end of the scene. Not a legend machine. An inheritance from the people who built this place and lost it.

**Turn:** Mercy carries something new — not knowledge exactly, but weight.

### Scene 6.2 — Young Darius
Among the archive material in the Sanctuary — footage, logs, records of the free era's organizing — Mercy finds a face he recognizes. Darius, young, in this same era. Same origin, same nothing. He was here. He was one of them.

Mercy puts it together without being told. Something happened to Darius that the free era couldn't hold — a grief, a failure, a loss. He chose power over that grief. He chose to own the thing that crushed him rather than keep fighting it.

The dark mirror lands: Darius is not a villain from outside the story. He's a possible destination. Same starting point, same world, same hunger for something the solar city didn't want to give him. Different choice.

Mercy sits with this quietly. He doesn't say it out loud.

**Turn:** He knows what he could become. He doesn't believe he will. He's wrong.
