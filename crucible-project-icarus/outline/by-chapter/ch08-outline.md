# Chapter 8 — Dead Air

**Movement:** III — The Scattering
**Beats:** 17 (Hardening Begins) · 18 (Beloved Endangered)
**Strand Focus:** Fire / Constellation
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 4,500–5,500

---

## Purpose

The hardening becomes visible — Mercy makes a choice that costs his humanity, and knows it, and keeps moving. Then Darius demonstrates leverage: the people Mercy loves are known. The two events in the same chapter show the feedback loop: the hunger's wake creates the exposure that creates the leverage.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 8.1 — The Calculation
A run goes wrong mid-execution. Someone from the activist network — not Mercy's core crew, a peripheral person who got too close to the operation — is in a position where protecting them and completing the run are mutually exclusive. The math is real: one or the other.

Mercy runs the calculation fast and keeps moving. He tells himself it was the only option. It wasn't. There was a slower path, a riskier one, one that would have cost him the run window. He chose the window.

The run completes. He lands clean. He doesn't mention the choice to Juno. The hardening is in how quickly he decided — the hunger doesn't pause for the cost.

**Turn:** Clean landing. Nobody asks. He doesn't tell.

### Scene 8.2 — Darius's Message
Darius's people come for Juno. Not violence — a visit. She's brought somewhere, shown something she wasn't supposed to see, and sent back. The message is architectural: *you have things Mercy values, and we know exactly what they are.*

Juno returns and tells Mercy exactly what happened. She doesn't soften it. She reports it cleanly, the way she handles everything she doesn't want to make worse.

Mercy's rage is immediate and useless. There's nowhere to put it inside the empire he already works for. Every threat to Juno is underwritten by the same runs he's been completing.

**Turn:** He connects the two events — Ch 8.1 and Ch 8.2 are the same mechanism. The hunger creates the wake, the wake creates the exposure, the exposure creates the leverage.

### Scene 8.3 — After
The chapter closes on Mercy alone. Two things happened today: he let someone go, and Juno was threatened. He made both possible.

He doesn't stop. But something in him hardens into a different shape — not the absence of feeling, but feeling organized into utility. He files it and moves on.

**Turn:** He keeps moving. The hardening is the chapter's last image.

## Tone
Two distinct sections. The calculation scene is tight and fast — the mid-run decision should feel like a microsecond even if it takes a page. The aftermath should be slower: Juno's report, Mercy's useless rage, the quiet after. The hardening happens in the gap between those two registers.
