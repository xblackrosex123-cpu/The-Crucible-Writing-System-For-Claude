# Chapter 16 — Obsolescence

**Movement:** IV — Brightest Burning
**Beats:** 28 (Third Forge Point — FP3)
**Strand Focus:** Quest / Fire / Constellation — all three in crisis
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 5,500–6,500
**Forge Point:** FP3 — Third Crucible
**Mercy Engine:** Mercy 3 — Zel

---

## Purpose

The deepest sacrifice before the finale. Darius answers the refusal with everything at once. The empire moves, the movement bleeds, Kweku runs out of careful. Mercy 3 — the last operational resource spent on Zel. Juno gets the truth. The constellation intact but changed, seeing Mercy clearly. He has one run left.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 16.1 — The Empire Moves (Quest crisis / Fire crisis)
Both notifications arrive at once. Deliberately timed.

Mercy's contract is cut — no runs, no income, no operational cover for anyone around him. He's out of the empire entirely.

In the same window: the new prototype wings fly publicly for the first time. Darius's replacement. In the upper lanes, in sanctioned airspace, performing runs that look like what Mercy could do six months ago. The message is architectural: *you were a tool. The tool has been replaced.*

**Fire crisis:** The fire shows him what he's losing. Not just the runs — the sky. The new wings are in the air and they aren't his. For the first time in the book, the hunger has no object. The sky he made is flying something else.

**Turn:** Obsolescence announced publicly. The sky he built belongs to the empire.

### Scene 16.2 — The Movement Pays (Constellation crisis)
Darius's sweep reaches the activist network. A key movement organizer is killed — someone Zel built the signal around, someone the lower decks knew and relied on. Not Zel. A death that lands in the movement's center.

Mercy 3: the sweep is coming for Zel. Darius's people have the broadcaster in their sights — the signal that's been amplifying everything, that made Mercy Moon into a story. Mercy intervenes with whatever operational resource he has left. Pulls Zel out of the path. It works. Zel survives.

It costs Mercy his last operational resource. He's out of moves.

**Sacrifice:** The last operational resource, spent protecting the person who believed in him.

**Turn:** Zel survives. A key organizer is dead. Mercy is out of everything except the wings and what he knows.

### Scene 16.3 — Kweku's Final Plea
Kweku comes to Mercy after the sweep. He's been building the escape plan, watching this all build, making the careful argument for years. He runs out of careful.

This is not Ch 4's quiet data presentation. This is Kweku having carried this since the free era and seeing it happen again. The final plea: stop, step back, let the plan do what the plan can do. The plan exists. It doesn't require Mercy to die for it.

Mercy says: *I know what I'm doing.*

Kweku stops talking. The silence is the same as Ch 4 — but further in, heavier. He's done this before. With someone else. In the free era. He knows how it ends. He stops because he knows stopping is the only thing left to do.

**Turn:** Kweku stops. The mentor bond holds — but Kweku is already grieving.

### Scene 16.4 — Juno
Juno learns the full truth about Ch 8 — what Mercy let happen, who got left behind — from the operational fallout, not from Mercy. She has the information now.

She doesn't confront him. She doesn't ask him to explain. She looks at him in a way she hasn't before — the full accounting visible in her face — and goes back to work.

The constellation is intact. Everyone who loves him now sees him clearly. None of them leave.

**Turn:** The chapter ends on Mercy alone. Stripped. Cut. The movement bleeding. The wings still running. He has one run left. He knows it.

## Tone
The most operationally dense chapter in Movement IV. Four scenes, four crises, one sacrifice. The pace should be relentless — events arriving faster than Mercy can respond to them. The Kweku scene is the chapter's emotional core; slow down there. The Juno scene is brief and should hit precisely because it's brief.
