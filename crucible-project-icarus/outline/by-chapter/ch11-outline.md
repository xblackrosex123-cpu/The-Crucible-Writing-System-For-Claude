# Chapter 11 — Burning Clean

**Movement:** III — The Scattering
**Beats:** 21 (Second Forge Point — FP2) · 22 (Thread Returns)
**Strand Focus:** Quest / Fire / Constellation — all three in crisis
**POV:** Mercy Moon
**Estimated Words:** 5,500–6,500
**Forge Point:** FP2 — Second Crucible

---

## Purpose

Close Movement III with the second impossible choice. The selfish burn — for the crowd, against Bex, no mission justification. Then Juno with both hidden things at once. Both lands define the Scattering's cost. Thread Returns surfaces Kweku's project and closes the FP0 conscript thread.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 11.1 — The Selfish Burn (FP2)
A public run — Bex is present in sanctioned lanes, the crowd is watching through Zel's signal. Mercy goes past the safe limit for the crowd and for Bex. Not for the mission. For himself.

The run is extraordinary. He wins in a way that shouldn't be survivable. The crowd through Zel's signal sees something they'll talk about for years. He beats Bex publicly, in view, in a way that cannot be explained by the legitimate equipment Bex flies.

**Fire crisis:** The first truly selfish full burn. Not the empire, not the escape plan, not protection. The hunger fed directly. The wings demand everything and he gives it. The body cost on landing is worse than anything before.

**Quest crisis:** The run's actual mission loses ground — he used the operational window for the spectacle. A line in Darius's ledger becomes something harder.

**Turn:** He lands. He won. The mission lost ground. He knows both.

### Scene 11.2 — Juno (FP2 continued)
Juno finds out both things in the same conversation:

Ch 8's abandoned activist — the person he let go, protected the run at their expense — she's known something happened. Now she knows what. He never told her.

Ch 10's uncredited cost — what covering his missed window actually cost her. She absorbed it. He didn't ask if she could.

**Constellation crisis:** She almost walks. Not in anger — in exhaustion. She has been the ground while he's been the sky, and the ground has limits. The word "almost" is doing the most work in the chapter.

Mercy doesn't argue. He listens — fully, actually present, not reframing or defending. For the first time in the book he receives someone's accounting of what he's cost them without turning away from it.

She doesn't leave. The almost-leaving changes the shape of what they are.

**Sacrifice:** The mission loses ground; the body cost is undeniable; Juno stays but the shape has changed.

**Turn:** She goes back to work. The shape between them is different.

### Scene 11.3 — Thread Returns (Beat 22)
Two threads return:

Kweku's parallel project surfaces: he's been building a plan — not just a modification but an actual route outside the empire entirely. A way the wings could function free of Darius. It's not ready. But it exists, and Mercy now knows it exists.

The crew member conscripted after FP0 reappears — changed, worn by the years of what was taken from them in Ch 2. Their reappearance is a reminder: the consequences of that first public race have been running this entire time.

**Turn:** Movement III closes. The escape plan is real. The FP0 cost is still in the room.
