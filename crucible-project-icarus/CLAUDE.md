- Dark Mirror: Darius — "You either become the sun or you become its fuel"
- 20 chapters, single POV (Mercy Moon, except Coda = Juno)
- Bebop-style chapter titles (oblique, not plot-summary)
- Opening frame: wreckage near the sun, no identity, hard cut to 6 years earlier
- Ending: Mercy climbs past any altitude ever reached, smiles before the wings fail, falls toward the sun
- Coda: Juno's POV, the flame implied to pass to someone new
