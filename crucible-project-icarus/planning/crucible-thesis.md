## Original Premise

In a solar-system city where criminal syndicates control the flight lanes, a brilliant young Black pilot from the lower decks becomes the face of a new drug-running empire after stealing prototype wings that let him outrun any ship alive. His mentor built the tech to escape the system, but he wants more than escape. He wants the sky to know his name. Inspired by the myth of Icarus — a slow burn to triumph, then a spectacular, inevitable fall.
